## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Focus on Patterns, Not Memorization**
   - Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Fast & Slow Pointers
   - BFS/DFS, Backtracking
   - Dynamic Programming patterns

2. **Quality Over Quantity**
   - Better to solve 1 problem deeply than 10 superficially
   - Always find the optimal solution

3. **Time Yourself**
   - Easy: 15-20 min
   - Medium: 25-35 min
   - Hard: 40-50 min

4. **Explain Out Loud**
   - Practice explaining your approach before coding
   - This is crucial for actual interviews

5. **Don't Give Up Too Early**
   - Struggle for 30 min before looking at hints
   - Struggling builds problem-solving muscle

## 🎯 Daily Best Practices

1. **Morning Routine (30 min)**:
   - Review yesterday's problems
   - Watch concept video if new topic

2. **Practice Session (2-3 hours)**:
   - Attempt problems without looking at solutions
   - If stuck for 30+ minutes, look at hints only
   - Implement solution
   - Optimize and analyze complexity

3. **Evening Review (30 min)**:
   - Review solutions on LeetCode Discuss
   - Note down patterns and techniques
   - Update personal problem log

4. **Weekend Strategy**:
   - Catch up on incomplete problems
   - Take one longer problem session
   - Review weak topics from the week

## 🔥 MAANG Interview Tips

1. **Always clarify the problem** before coding
2. **Think out loud** - explain your thought process
3. **Start with brute force**, then optimize
4. **Test with edge cases** before submitting
5. **Analyze time and space complexity** for every solution
6. **Ask questions** about constraints and requirements
7. **Practice on whiteboard** or paper without IDE
8. **Communicate clearly** - more important than perfect code
